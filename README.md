# AI Business Ideas Across 21 Professional Fields

315 AI business ideas, 15 for each of 21 professional fields. Every idea is worked out in the same structure: the buyer, the problem, the platform and key screens, core functionality, the AI and human review, MVP scope, defensibility, test pricing, the first 30 days of marketing, the paid pilot, metrics and operating controls.

Browse them with visuals, and apply to build one, at **[nexibeo.com/ideas](https://nexibeo.com/ideas/#fields)**.

## How to use this

Take any idea and build it. Before you do, read its research status: demand, pricing, differentiation, build scope and integration feasibility are hypotheses to test, not verified market findings. Each idea page includes a paid pilot plan for testing them.

[Nexibeo](https://nexibeo.com) designs, builds and runs AI brands. Each idea's page on nexibeo.com has more detail on estimation, and a form to co-create it with us.
