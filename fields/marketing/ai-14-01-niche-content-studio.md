# Niche content studio

**Marketing** · Also fits: Creatives; Sales; Writers

![Niche content studio](visuals/niche-content-studio.svg)

> For marketing managers at specialist manufacturing suppliers, turn expert interviews, product documentation and editorial briefs into reviewed niche content package. Address the recurring problem: writers lack the technical context needed for useful content. The value hypothesis is a more complete, reviewable deliverable with less repeated preparation; the pilot must establish whether that benefit is real.

| **Problem** | Writers lack the technical context needed for useful content. |
| **Format** | Source-based content workspace with editorial delivery |
| **USP** | Subject-matter interviews and source checking built into recurring production. |

## The product
Key screens: Topic calendar, source editor, expert review. Use a project list and editorial calendar beside a document editor. Keep original material and supporting passages in a collapsible side panel. Show outline, draft, review and approved stages. Provide tracked edits, comments, version comparisons and an export preview that reflects the final delivery format. In this product, the first view is topic calendar, followed by source editor and expert review.

## Core functionality
1. Map audience questions.
2. Extract expert insights.
3. Draft articles.
4. Check technical claims.
5. Adapt channel formats.
6. Manage expert approvals.

## Customer workflow
Collect a structured brief and source material, identify unanswered questions, approve an outline, generate a draft, verify claims, gather reviewer edits, approve a final version and export the agreed formats. Start with expert interviews, product documentation and editorial briefs and finish with reviewed niche content package.

## AI and human review
Extract and organize information, propose structure, draft prose and adapt approved content to audiences. Attach evidence to factual claims. Keep names, dates, amounts and quoted wording linked to their source. Editors resolve ambiguity and approve publication.

## Customer inputs
Expert interviews, product documentation and editorial briefs

## Customer deliverables
Reviewed niche content package

## Accounts and administration
Client workspaces, source permissions, editorial assignments, change history, reviewer comments, approval gates, revision allowances and export templates.

## MVP scope
Begin with marketing managers at specialist manufacturing suppliers and one recurring use case. Build the first two modules: map audience questions; extract expert insights. Provide operator assistance for the third module: draft articles. Deliver reviewed niche content package through a manual review queue. Perform other necessary full-scope functions manually during the pilot. Include all applicable access, accuracy and professional-review controls from the start.

## After MVP validation
After paid pilots establish value, automate the remaining modules: check technical claims; adapt channel formats; manage expert approvals. Add one validated source integration, reusable customer configuration and recurring delivery. Expand to additional teams, document formats or languages only after testing the new scope.

## Build dependencies
Document parsing, a source-linked editor, tracked revisions, reviewer workflow and reliable document export. Rich presentation or print output needs format-specific QA.

## Integrations and data access
Approved brand material, campaign exports and authorized customer research. Document storage, word processor export, content management systems and approved publishing channels. Pilot with uploads and downloadable drafts before adding write integrations. These are candidate integration categories, not verified supported connectors.

## Defensibility
Customer-approved terminology, reusable structures, source libraries and editorial feedback tied to a specific audience and recurring publishing workflow. For this idea, build around subject-matter interviews and source checking built into recurring production. This advantage requires execution and accumulated customer trust; the base model alone is not a defensible asset.

## Alternatives and positioning
Writers, editors, agencies, internal document templates and general-purpose chat tools. Differentiate on this specific proposed advantage: subject-matter interviews and source checking built into recurring production. Test it against the buyer's current method on the same task. Competitor coverage and uniqueness have not been established.

## Revenue model and test pricing (USD)
Test USD 400-1,500 for a tightly scoped initial content package. Convert repeated work to a monthly retainer with explicit deliverable and revision limits. Specialist review and substantial research are separately scoped. Prices are hypotheses.

## Main delivery costs
Research and interview time, transcription, model usage, factual verification, subject-matter review, editing and revisions.

## First 30 days of marketing
- Week 1: interview five prospective buyers in this segment: marketing managers at specialist manufacturing suppliers. Ask to see a recent example of the problem and their current process.
- Week 2: prepare this demonstration using authorized or synthetic material: an expert-reviewed article from one technical interview.
- Week 3: present it through industry marketing agencies and seek one narrowly scoped paid pilot.
- Week 4: review expert correction rate, qualified reader engagement, total delivery effort and a concrete renewal decision before increasing scope.

## Paid pilot and validation
Complete one existing brief using the customer’s actual sources. Record reviewer edits, factual corrections and preparation time. Ask the same buyer to commission a second comparable deliverable. For this idea, use expert interviews, product documentation and editorial briefs and evaluate reviewed niche content package. Agree success thresholds with the buyer before starting; collect a baseline for expert correction rate, qualified reader engagement. A positive signal is payment and repeat use with acceptable quality and delivery cost, not a favorable demo reaction alone.

## Success metrics
Expert correction rate, qualified reader engagement

## Retention and expansion
Maintain the approved source and voice library, schedule recurring editorial work, and expand into additional formats only after the core deliverable is repeatedly accepted.

## Operating controls and limitations
Verify product claims and permissions. Distinguish observed campaign results from causal explanations and keep customer data collection authorized. Validate source access and reviewer availability during the pilot. Maintain customer-level access, data deletion controls and a record of final approvals.
