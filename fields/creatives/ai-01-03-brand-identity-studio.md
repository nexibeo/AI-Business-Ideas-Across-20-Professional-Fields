# Brand identity studio

**AI-01-03 · Creatives** · Also fits: Marketing; Sales

![Brand identity studio](visuals/brand-identity-studio.svg)

> For founders launching specialist service businesses, turn founder brief, audience description and visual references into editable brand guide and coordinated starter assets. Address the recurring problem: freelance deliverables lack a coherent reusable brand system. The value hypothesis is a more complete, reviewable deliverable with less repeated preparation; the pilot must establish whether that benefit is real.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Buyer** | Founders launching specialist service businesses |
| **Problem** | Freelance deliverables lack a coherent reusable brand system. |
| **Format** | Visual production platform with managed creative review |
| **USP** | A usable identity system tied to positioning and real customer touchpoints. |

## The product
Key screens: Discovery board, identity canvas, brand kit. Use a thumbnail gallery for projects, a large central editing canvas, and a right-hand panel for references, constraints and comments. Let users compare versions side by side. Display draft, changes requested and approved states. Provide a client preview link with comments anchored to the relevant asset. In this product, the first view is discovery board, followed by identity canvas and brand kit.

## Core functionality
1. Capture positioning choices.
2. Explore visual directions.
3. Refine logo concepts.
4. Define typography and colors.
5. Generate application mockups.
6. Package editable guidelines.

## Customer workflow
Create a brief, upload reference material, choose constraints, review a small set of directions, refine the selected direction, request client approval, and export a versioned delivery pack. Start with founder brief, audience description and visual references and finish with editable brand guide and coordinated starter assets.

## AI and human review
Use language models to interpret briefs and visual models where appropriate to generate or adapt assets. Keep factual attributes in structured fields. Validate dimensions and export formats through deterministic checks. A creative reviewer confirms visual quality and fidelity before delivery.

## Customer inputs
Founder brief, audience description and visual references

## Customer deliverables
Editable brand guide and coordinated starter assets

## Accounts and administration
Project ownership, asset versions, client comments, approval states, usage allowances, revision limits, download history and a rights record for supplied material.

## MVP scope
Begin with founders launching specialist service businesses and one recurring use case. Build the first two modules: capture positioning choices; explore visual directions. Provide operator assistance for the third module: refine logo concepts. Deliver editable brand guide and coordinated starter assets through a manual review queue. Perform other necessary full-scope functions manually during the pilot. Include all applicable access, accuracy and professional-review controls from the start.

## After MVP validation
After paid pilots establish value, automate the remaining modules: define typography and colors; generate application mockups; package editable guidelines. Add one validated source integration, reusable customer configuration and recurring delivery. Expand to additional teams, document formats or languages only after testing the new scope.

## Build dependencies
Asset upload and preview, asynchronous generation jobs, editable version history, reviewer access and tested export formats. High-fidelity production requires specialist creative QA.

## Integrations and data access
Existing artwork, campaign systems and client approval processes. Cloud asset storage, design-file import/export and publishing destinations. Start with file exchange and validate destination specifications before promising direct publishing. These are candidate integration categories, not verified supported connectors.

## Defensibility
A reusable library of approved styles, production constraints and review examples, together with reliable delivery for a narrow creative niche. For this idea, build around a usable identity system tied to positioning and real customer touchpoints. This advantage requires execution and accumulated customer trust; the base model alone is not a defensible asset.

## Alternatives and positioning
Freelancers, creative agencies, generic generation tools and existing design applications. Differentiate on this specific proposed advantage: a usable identity system tied to positioning and real customer touchpoints. Test it against the buyer's current method on the same task. Competitor coverage and uniqueness have not been established.

## Revenue model and test pricing (USD)
Test a USD 300-1,500 fixed pilot for one defined asset package. Offer a monthly production allowance after repeat demand. Quote complex video, 3D or specialist design separately. These are test prices, not market benchmarks.

## Main delivery costs
Generation attempts, video or image processing, storage, reviewer hours, client revision rounds and licensed source assets.

Startup incubators and specialist business launch consultants

A brand consistency teardown with two visual directions

## First 30 days of marketing
- Week 1: interview five prospective buyers in this segment: founders launching specialist service businesses. Ask to see a recent example of the problem and their current process.
- Week 2: prepare this demonstration using authorized or synthetic material: a brand consistency teardown with two visual directions.
- Week 3: present it through startup incubators and specialist business launch consultants and seek one narrowly scoped paid pilot.
- Week 4: review revision rounds, paid brand kit conversion, total delivery effort and a concrete renewal decision before increasing scope.

## Paid pilot and validation
Deliver one real creative brief with a capped asset count and revision allowance. Compare usable deliverables, reviewer time and client acceptance with the existing production method. For this idea, use founder brief, audience description and visual references and evaluate editable brand guide and coordinated starter assets. Agree success thresholds with the buyer before starting; collect a baseline for revision rounds, paid brand kit conversion. A positive signal is payment and repeat use with acceptable quality and delivery cost, not a favorable demo reaction alone.

## Success metrics
Revision rounds, paid brand kit conversion

## Retention and expansion
Save approved styles and constraints, offer repeat production batches, and expand only into adjacent asset formats the same buyer already commissions.

## Operating controls and limitations
Protect supplied asset rights, client approvals and product fidelity. Do not reuse private client assets across accounts. Validate source access and reviewer availability during the pilot. Maintain customer-level access, data deletion controls and a record of final approvals.
