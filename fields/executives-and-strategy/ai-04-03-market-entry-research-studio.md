## The product
Key screens: Research questions, evidence map, entry options. Organize work by research question. Show a source library, an evidence matrix and a draft findings panel with linked quotations. Keep contradictory findings and unanswered questions visible. Allow reviewers to inspect the original context before accepting an interpretation. In this product, the first view is research questions, followed by evidence map and entry options.

## Core functionality
1. Define target segments.
2. Map local alternatives.
3. Compare channels.
4. Document entry constraints.
5. Distinguish evidence from assumptions.
6. Assemble decision briefs.

## Customer workflow
Agree the decision and research questions, define permitted sources or participants, collect evidence, code findings, compare supporting and contradictory material, review interpretations, and deliver a cited brief with next questions. Start with public market sources, customer interviews and product constraints and finish with market entry evidence pack and options brief.

## AI and human review
Assist with retrieval, transcription, structured extraction and thematic synthesis. Preserve source passages and methodological context. Human researchers validate inclusion, quotations and conclusions. Use real participants when customer research is required.

## Customer inputs
Public market sources, customer interviews and product constraints

## Customer deliverables
Market entry evidence pack and options brief

## Accounts and administration
Source provenance, participant consent where applicable, research questions, coding definitions, reviewer disagreements, citations and versioned conclusions.

## MVP scope
Begin with export managers at specialist manufacturers and one recurring use case. Build the first two modules: define target segments; map local alternatives. Provide operator assistance for the third module: compare channels. Deliver market entry evidence pack and options brief through a manual review queue. Perform other necessary full-scope functions manually during the pilot. Include all applicable access, accuracy and professional-review controls from the start.

## After MVP validation
After paid pilots establish value, automate the remaining modules: document entry constraints; distinguish evidence from assumptions; assemble decision briefs. Add one validated source integration, reusable customer configuration and recurring delivery. Expand to additional teams, document formats or languages only after testing the new scope.

## Build dependencies
A clear research protocol, source access, citation tracking and qualified interpretation. Interview work also needs relevant participants and consent management.

## Paid pilot and validation
Answer one practical question using a bounded evidence set. Ask a domain expert to review citations and reasoning, identify contrary evidence and assess whether the deliverable supports the intended decision. For this idea, use public market sources, customer interviews and product constraints and evaluate market entry evidence pack and options brief. Agree success thresholds with the buyer before starting; collect a baseline for decision-relevant findings, repeat research orders. A positive signal is payment and repeat use with acceptable quality and delivery cost, not a favorable demo reaction alone.

## Success metrics
Decision-relevant findings, repeat research orders

## Retention and expansion
Maintain the research question and evidence archive, offer follow-up studies and refresh important sources. Build repeat work around the buyer’s decision cycle.

## Operating controls and limitations
Show source dates and distinguish evidence from strategic assumptions. Keep sensitive company plans restricted to authorized participants. Validate source access and reviewer availability during the pilot. Maintain customer-level access, data deletion controls and a record of final approvals.
