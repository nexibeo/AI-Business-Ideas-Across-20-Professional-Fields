# Certificate request coordinator

**Insurance** · Also fits: Operations; Customer Support; Management

![Certificate request coordinator](visuals/certificate-request-coordinator.svg)

> For commercial insurance service teams, turn client requests and agency-approved request templates into structured certificate requests. Address the recurring problem: certificate requests lack recipient details and clear requirements. The value hypothesis is a more complete, reviewable deliverable with less repeated preparation; the pilot must establish whether that benefit is real.

## The product
Key screens: Request portal, missing details, authorized staff queue. Use a queue or timeline as the opening view, with clear owners, dates and current states. Each case opens into its source context, proposed actions and discussion. Give external participants a limited form or status page. Make the next required action visible without opening every record. In this product, the first view is request portal, followed by missing details and authorized staff queue.

## Core functionality
1. Collect recipient details.
2. Extract requested wording.
3. Flag unusual requirements.
4. Identify missing information.
5. Route authorized staff.
6. Track completion.

## Customer workflow
Create a case from approved inputs, identify required actions, confirm owners and dates, request missing information, approve proposed communications or changes, track completion, and retain a handover record. Start with client requests and agency-approved request templates and finish with structured certificate requests.

## AI and human review
Extract candidate actions and relevant context, classify requests and draft communications. Use explicit rules for scheduling, state transitions and permissions. People confirm obligations and approve consequential actions before execution.

## Customer inputs
Client requests and agency-approved request templates

## Customer deliverables
Structured certificate requests

## Accounts and administration
Role permissions, task ownership, deadlines, reminders, approval gates, exception handling, action history, duplicate prevention and reversible configuration.

## MVP scope
Begin with commercial insurance service teams and one recurring use case. Build the first two modules: collect recipient details; extract requested wording. Provide operator assistance for the third module: flag unusual requirements. Deliver structured certificate requests through a manual review queue. Perform other necessary full-scope functions manually during the pilot. Include all applicable access, accuracy and professional-review controls from the start.

## After MVP validation
After paid pilots establish value, automate the remaining modules: identify missing information; route authorized staff; track completion. Add one validated source integration, reusable customer configuration and recurring delivery. Expand to additional teams, document formats or languages only after testing the new scope.

## Build dependencies
Explicit state definitions, owner mapping, approval rules, idempotent actions, notifications and recovery procedures. Workflow reliability matters more than fluent text.

## Integrations and data access
Broker-approved policy documents, case records and carrier requirements. Calendars, email, task managers and relevant business records. Use draft actions and supervised handoffs first, then enable only specifically authorized writes. These are candidate integration categories, not verified supported connectors.

## Defensibility
Customer-specific workflow rules, reliable handoffs, operational history and integrations that make the service part of daily work. For this idea, build around complete requests with unusual wording highlighted before certificate preparation. This advantage requires execution and accumulated customer trust; the base model alone is not a defensible asset.

Competitor coverage and uniqueness have not been established.

## Paid pilot and validation
Run one recurring workflow with a small team. Track every handoff and exception, verify completed actions, and compare handling time and missed commitments with the prior process. For this idea, use client requests and agency-approved request templates and evaluate structured certificate requests. Agree success thresholds with the buyer before starting; collect a baseline for clarification rounds, request turnaround. A positive signal is payment and repeat use with acceptable quality and delivery cost, not a favorable demo reaction alone.

## Success metrics
Clarification rounds, request turnaround

## Retention and expansion
Report completed actions and recurring exceptions. Maintain the workflow as policies change, then add one adjacent handoff with clear ownership.

## Operating controls and limitations
Separate document preparation from coverage, underwriting and claims decisions. Authorized professionals review policy meaning and customer commitments. Validate source access and reviewer availability during the pilot. Maintain customer-level access, data deletion controls and a record of final approvals.
