# Deal review briefing

**Sales** · Also fits: Marketing; Operations; Science and Research

![Deal review briefing](visuals/deal-review-briefing.svg)

> For sales managers running weekly pipeline reviews, turn confirmed CRM records and authorized sales activity into deal review and next-step brief. Address the recurring problem: opportunity updates hide missing buyer evidence and commitments. The value hypothesis is a more complete, reviewable deliverable with less repeated preparation; the pilot must establish whether that benefit is real.

## The product
Key screens: Deal overview, evidence gaps, next actions. Open with a compact overview and filters for the relevant period or segment. Let users drill from each theme or metric into underlying records. Keep source definitions and missing-data notes near the result. Use an action panel to assign investigations and record what was learned. In this product, the first view is deal overview, followed by evidence gaps and next actions.

## Core functionality
1. Summarize stated needs.
2. Extract confirmed commitments.
3. Flag missing stakeholders.
4. Distinguish assumptions.
5. Highlight stalled steps.
6. Prepare manager questions.

## Customer workflow
Agree definitions, import authorized data, validate coverage and identifiers, compute transparent measures, group relevant evidence, review findings, assign investigations or improvements, and repeat on a comparable period. Start with confirmed CRM records and authorized sales activity and finish with deal review and next-step brief.

## AI and human review
Classify text, summarize evidence and propose explanations to investigate. Compute financial or operational measures with deterministic code. Separate observed patterns from causal claims and preserve examples that contradict the summary.

## Customer inputs
Confirmed CRM records and authorized sales activity

## Customer deliverables

## Accounts and administration
Dataset permissions, field mappings, metric definitions, source drill-down, saved filters, reviewer annotations, recurring reports and action ownership.

## MVP scope
Begin with sales managers running weekly pipeline reviews and one recurring use case. Build the first two modules: summarize stated needs; extract confirmed commitments. Provide operator assistance for the third module: flag missing stakeholders. Deliver deal review and next-step brief through a manual review queue. Perform other necessary full-scope functions manually during the pilot. Include all applicable access, accuracy and professional-review controls from the start.

## After MVP validation
After paid pilots establish value, automate the remaining modules: distinguish assumptions; highlight stalled steps; prepare manager questions. Add one validated source integration, reusable customer configuration and recurring delivery. Expand to additional teams, document formats or languages only after testing the new scope.

## Build dependencies
Stable identifiers, consistent metric definitions, deterministic calculations, source lineage and representative review samples. Poor coverage must remain visible.

## Integrations and data access
Approved sales collateral, CRM records and product or pricing information. Read-only business data exports, reporting databases and task trackers. Reconcile source totals before scheduling recurring data refreshes. These are candidate integration categories, not verified supported connectors.

## Retention and expansion
Repeat the same definitions each reporting period and track whether findings lead to useful action. Expand data sources without breaking historical comparability.

## Operating controls and limitations
Keep product capabilities and commercial terms verified. Use authorized customer records and require review before outreach, promises or pricing exceptions. Validate source access and reviewer availability during the pilot. Maintain customer-level access, data deletion controls and a record of final approvals.
