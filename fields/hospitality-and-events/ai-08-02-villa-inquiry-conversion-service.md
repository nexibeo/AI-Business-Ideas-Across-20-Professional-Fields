# Villa inquiry conversion service

**Hospitality and Events** · Also fits: Customer Support; Sales; Management

![Villa inquiry conversion service](visuals/villa-inquiry-conversion-service.svg)

> For managers operating several holiday villas, turn verified availability, villa details and offer rules into personalized stay proposals and follow-up tasks. Address the recurring problem: slow and incomplete inquiry replies lose interested guests. The value hypothesis is a more complete, reviewable deliverable with less repeated preparation; the pilot must establish whether that benefit is real.

| | |

| **Buyer** | Managers operating several holiday villas |
| **Problem** | Slow and incomplete inquiry replies lose interested guests. |
| **Format** | Operational coordination portal |
| **USP** | Property matching and complete stay proposals in one inquiry workflow. |

## The product
Key screens: Inquiry inbox, stay proposal, follow-up calendar. Use a queue or timeline as the opening view, with clear owners, dates and current states. Each case opens into its source context, proposed actions and discussion. Give external participants a limited form or status page. Make the next required action visible without opening every record. In this product, the first view is inquiry inbox, followed by stay proposal and follow-up calendar.

## Core functionality
1. Collect dates and party size.
2. Check approved availability.
3. Compare suitable villas.
4. Draft stay offers.
5. Schedule authorized follow-ups.
6. Track outcomes.

## Customer workflow
Create a case from approved inputs, identify required actions, confirm owners and dates, request missing information, approve proposed communications or changes, track completion, and retain a handover record. Start with verified availability, villa details and offer rules and finish with personalized stay proposals and follow-up tasks.

## AI and human review
Extract candidate actions and relevant context, classify requests and draft communications. Use explicit rules for scheduling, state transitions and permissions. People confirm obligations and approve consequential actions before execution.

## Customer inputs
Verified availability, villa details and offer rules

## Customer deliverables
Personalized stay proposals and follow-up tasks

## Accounts and administration
Role permissions, task ownership, deadlines, reminders, approval gates, exception handling, action history, duplicate prevention and reversible configuration.

## MVP scope
Begin with managers operating several holiday villas and one recurring use case. Build the first two modules: collect dates and party size; check approved availability. Provide operator assistance for the third module: compare suitable villas. Deliver personalized stay proposals and follow-up tasks through a manual review queue. Perform other necessary full-scope functions manually during the pilot. Include all applicable access, accuracy and professional-review controls from the start.

## After MVP validation
After paid pilots establish value, automate the remaining modules: draft stay offers; schedule authorized follow-ups; track outcomes. Add one validated source integration, reusable customer configuration and recurring delivery. Expand to additional teams, document formats or languages only after testing the new scope.

## Build dependencies
Explicit state definitions, owner mapping, approval rules, idempotent actions, notifications and recovery procedures. Workflow reliability matters more than fluent text.

## Integrations and data access
Property records, event schedules, reservation exports and supplier information. Calendars, email, task managers and relevant business records. Use draft actions and supervised handoffs first, then enable only specifically authorized writes. These are candidate integration categories, not verified supported connectors.

## Defensibility
Customer-specific workflow rules, reliable handoffs, operational history and integrations that make the service part of daily work. For this idea, build around property matching and complete stay proposals in one inquiry workflow. This advantage requires execution and accumulated customer trust; the base model alone is not a defensible asset.

## Alternatives and positioning
Shared inboxes, spreadsheets, task boards and existing workflow automation products. Differentiate on this specific proposed advantage: property matching and complete stay proposals in one inquiry workflow. Test it against the buyer's current method on the same task. Competitor coverage and uniqueness have not been established.

## Revenue model and test pricing (USD)
Test USD 750-2,500 setup plus USD 200-800 monthly for one bounded workflow and team. Cap case volume and implementation scope. Larger operational integrations need separate quotes. Prices are hypotheses.

## Main delivery costs
Workflow configuration, integration maintenance, model calls, notification delivery, exception support and monitoring.

## First 30 days of marketing
- Week 1: interview five prospective buyers in this segment: managers operating several holiday villas. Ask to see a recent example of the problem and their current process.
- Week 2: prepare this demonstration using authorized or synthetic material: a sample inquiry-to-stay proposal journey.
- Week 3: present it through villa management networks and seek one narrowly scoped paid pilot.
- Week 4: review response time, inquiry-to-booking conversion, total delivery effort and a concrete renewal decision before increasing scope.

## Paid pilot and validation
Run one recurring workflow with a small team. Track every handoff and exception, verify completed actions, and compare handling time and missed commitments with the prior process. For this idea, use verified availability, villa details and offer rules and evaluate personalized stay proposals and follow-up tasks. Agree success thresholds with the buyer before starting; collect a baseline for response time, inquiry-to-booking conversion. A positive signal is payment and repeat use with acceptable quality and delivery cost, not a favorable demo reaction alone.

## Success metrics
Response time, inquiry-to-booking conversion

## Retention and expansion
Report completed actions and recurring exceptions. Maintain the workflow as policies change, then add one adjacent handoff with clear ownership.

## Operating controls and limitations
Verify property facts, availability and supplier conditions. Staff approve commercial exceptions and consequential booking changes. Validate source access and reviewer availability during the pilot. Maintain customer-level access, data deletion controls and a record of final approvals.
